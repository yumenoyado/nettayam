Things to do in Trivandrum

A local host's guide to Thiruvananthapuram: temples and palaces, golden beaches, quiet backwaters, misty hills and the food you shouldn't miss.

Plan your days See our homestay

Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) is Kerala's capital and its southernmost city. It's greener and calmer than most state capitals, and within an hour or two you can be on a palm-fringed beach, in a boat on the backwaters or up in the hills of the Western Ghats.

We're Manjusha and Prakash, the hosts of Yume no Yado, a 2-bedroom homestay in quiet Nettayam. This is the advice we give our own guests. Distances are from our house, about 11 km from the airport.

Plan your trip

Suggested itineraries

1 day: the old city

  1. Morning: Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple (dress code applies) and Kuthira Malika Palace next door.
  2. Lunch: a Kerala meal on a banana leaf near East Fort.
  3. Afternoon: Napier Museum, the Sree Chitra Art Gallery and the zoo.
  4. Short on time? Take the KSRTC double-decker city tour instead. It covers most of these in one ride.
  5. Evening: sunset at Shangumugham Beach, then snacks around East Fort.

2 days: city + coast

  1. Day 1: follow the 1-day old-city plan.
  2. Day 2 morning: boat ride through the Poovar backwaters to the golden sandbar.
  3. Day 2 afternoon: Vizhinjam fishing harbour, then Kovalam: climb the lighthouse and stay for a seafood dinner by the beach.

4–5 days: southern Kerala

  1. Days 1–2: city and coast as above.
  2. Day 3: Ponmudi hills, stopping at Kallar on the way.
  3. Day 4: Varkala cliff and beach, with Jatayu Earth's Center on the same road.
  4. Day 5: Padmanabhapuram Palace and Kanyakumari (leave early, and avoid Mondays when the palace is closed).

Easy sightseeing

🚌 KSRTC open-top double-decker city tour

The easiest way to see Trivandrum's highlights in one go is the KSRTC "City Ride", an open-top double-decker bus tour run by the Kerala State Road Transport Corporation's Budget Tourism Cell. Sit on the upper deck for the best views of the old city, palaces and the sea.

  • Timings: runs daily from 3 PM to 10 PM. There's no separate day or night tour, so you see the sights in daylight and the city lit up after dark.
  • Route: Napier Museum, the zoo, the Science & Technology Museum and Planetarium, Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, the Secretariat, the Legislative Assembly, Kanakakunnu Palace, Shangumugham Beach and more.
  • Starts from: East Fort, at the KSRTC City depot opposite the North Bus Stand. That's about 11 km and 30 minutes from Yume no Yado by taxi.
  • 🎟️ Upper deck (open top)₹200
  • 🎟️ Lower deck₹100

You can also book in the Ente KSRTC Neo-OPRS app, or buy tickets on the bus if seats are free. Weekend and holiday rides fill up, so book a day ahead. Fares and timings change from time to time, so please confirm when booking. We're happy to help you book.

Places to visit

🏛️ In the city

🛕~12 km · 35 min

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

The temple the city is named after, with a towering seven-tiered gopuram and the famous reclining image of Lord Vishnu. One of the most important temples in Kerala.

Tip: Only Hindus may enter, and there is a strict dress code: mundu/dhoti for men (no shirt), saree or long skirt for women. Dhotis can be hired near the entrance.

🐎Next to the temple

Kuthira Malika Palace Museum

A palace built by Maharaja Swathi Thirunal in traditional Kerala style. Its eaves are supported by 122 carved wooden horses, which give it its name. Inside are royal Travancore treasures.

Tip: Closed on Mondays. Usually open mornings and afternoons, with a lunch break.

🖼️~8 km · 25 min

Napier Museum, Art Gallery & Zoo

A striking 19th-century museum of bronzes, jewellery and ivory carvings. The Sree Chitra Art Gallery holds paintings by Raja Ravi Varma, and the zoo is in the same leafy park.

Tip: Closed on Mondays. Go in the morning, when it's cooler and the animals are more active.

🛍️~11 km · 30 min

East Fort & Chalai Market

The bustling old-city bazaar next to the temple, good for spices, banana chips, brass lamps and saris. In the evening, try snacks and tea from the stalls around Kizhakkekotta (East Fort).

Tip: Go in the late afternoon to shop, then stay for evening snacks.

🧜~16 km · 40 min

Shangumugham Beach

The city's favourite beach for sunsets, home to a giant reclining mermaid sculpture. It's close to the airport, so it works well before an evening flight.

Tip: Best at sunset. The sea is rough, so it isn't safe for swimming.

🚣~16 km · 40 min

Veli Tourist Village

A park where Veli Lake meets the Arabian Sea, with pedal boats, a floating bridge, sculptures and gardens. Families with children enjoy it.

Tip: Open roughly 9 AM–6 PM. Late afternoon is pleasant.

⛪~15 km · 40 min

Vettukad Church & Church Museum

Madre de Deus Church at Vettukad, by the sea near Shangumugham and Veli, has a history going back centuries; St Francis Xavier is believed to have visited in 1544. It's famous for its tall statue of Christ the King, and a new church museum opened beside it in 2024.

Tip: Open daily, roughly 6 AM–10 PM, and visited by people of all faiths. The Feast of Christ the King in November draws huge crowds. Combine it with Shangumugham Beach or Veli.

🪔~14 km · 40 min

Attukal Bhagavathy Temple

A much-loved temple to the goddess Attukal Amma, famous for Attukal Pongala, when huge numbers of women cook offerings along the city's streets.

Tip: Visit early in the morning. On Pongala day the whole area is closed to traffic.

🏰~8 km · 25 min

Kanakakunnu Palace

A hilltop palace with wide lawns and old trees, next to Napier Museum. It hosts cultural events and festivals, and its grounds are a favourite spot for an evening walk.

Tip: Combine it with Napier Museum. Evenings are pleasant for a stroll.

🎯Under 1 km

Vattiyoorkavu Shooting Range

A world-class shooting range built for the 2015 National Games. It has hosted National Shooting Championships, and athletes come here to train and compete.

Tip: Coming for a camp or championship? Yume no Yado is less than 1 km away, with a kitchen and quiet rooms to rest between sessions.

🏖️ Beaches & backwaters

🌊~20 km · 45 min

Kovalam Beach

Kerala's best-known beach town: three crescent beaches (Lighthouse, Hawa and Samudra) with seafood restaurants and Ayurvedic massage. Climb the red-and-white lighthouse for the view.

Tip: Best from October to March, when the sea is calmer. Swim only where lifeguards are on duty.

⛵~24 km · 50 min

Vizhinjam

A colourful fishing harbour just south of Kovalam, with an ancient rock-cut cave temple. Combine it with a Kovalam visit.

Tip: Come early in the morning to see the boats come in with the day's catch.

🔱~28 km · 1 hr

Azhimala Shiva Temple

A seaside temple on the cliffs near Vizhinjam, known for its 58-foot (18 m) statue of Lord Shiva as Gangadhareshwara, the tallest Shiva sculpture in Kerala, set against the Arabian Sea.

Tip: Combine it with Kovalam, Vizhinjam or Poovar. Go towards sunset, and keep away from the cliff edges and rough sea.

🛶~35 km · 1 hr

Poovar Backwaters

Take a boat through mangrove-lined backwaters to the golden sandbar where the Neyyar River meets the sea. This is a classic Kerala backwater experience close to the city.

Tip: Boats leave from the jetties at Poovar. Mornings are calmer and cooler.

🌅~50 km · 1.5 hr

Varkala Cliff

Dramatic red laterite cliffs above Papanasam Beach, lined with cafés and shops. Nearby is the ancient Janardhana Swamy Temple.

Tip: Stay for the sunset from the North Cliff walkway.

⛰️ Hills, dams & nature

💧Nearby · ~8 km

Aruvikkara Dam

A small, peaceful reservoir and riverside temple just a short drive from the house. It's a quiet spot for a morning walk or an evening outing.

Tip: A nice short outing when you have an hour or two to spare.

🐊~25 km · 50 min

Neyyar Dam & Wildlife Sanctuary

A large reservoir framed by the Western Ghats, with boating and a crocodile rehabilitation centre. The Kappukadu elephant rehabilitation centre is nearby.

Tip: Arrive by 10 AM. The elephant centre is closed on Mondays.

🍃~50 km · 1.5–2 hr

Ponmudi Hill Station

A cool, misty hill station reached by a scenic road with 22 hairpin bends through forest and tea estates. You can see Golden Valley, waterfalls, viewpoints and trekking trails.

Tip: Best from November to February. Carry a light jacket. The road may close in heavy rain.

🌊~22 km · 50 min

Vellayani Lake

A large freshwater lake south of the city, lined with paddy fields and coconut palms. It's lovely at sunrise and sunset, and popular with photographers and birdwatchers.

Tip: Go at dawn or dusk for the best light and birds.

💦~40 km · 1–1.25 hr

Kallar & Meenmutty

A clear mountain river with rocky pools at Kallar, and the Meenmutty waterfall reached by a forest walk. Both are on the road to Ponmudi.

Tip: Stop here on your way up to Ponmudi. Avoid the river after heavy rain.

🚗 Day trips

🕉️~35 km · 1–1.25 hr

Chenkal Maheswaram Temple

The Sree Siva Parvathi Temple at Chenkal, near Neyyattinkara, is home to a 111-foot Shivalingam, one of the tallest in the world. You can walk up through the levels inside the lingam.

Tip: Combine it with Poovar or Azhimala on the same southern route. Follow the temple's dress code and check timings before you go.

🦅~55 km · 1.5 hr

Jatayu Earth's Center

A giant sculpture of the mythical bird Jatayu from the Ramayana, set on a rocky hilltop. A cable car takes you to the top, where there are wide views and adventure activities.

Tip: Book tickets online in advance, especially on weekends.

🏯~65 km · 1.5–2 hr

Padmanabhapuram Palace

The former seat of the Travancore kings, a 400-year-old masterpiece of Kerala woodwork with carved ceilings and polished floors. It's just across the border in Tamil Nadu.

Tip: Closed on Mondays. Combine it with Kanyakumari for a full day out.

🌄~100 km · 2.5–3 hr

Kanyakumari

The southern tip of India, where three seas meet. See the sunrise, take the ferry to the Vivekananda Rock Memorial and the Thiruvalluvar Statue, and visit the Kumari Amman Temple.

Tip: Leave very early to catch the sunrise, or go for the sunset.

Distances and drive times from Yume no Yado are approximate and depend on traffic. Opening hours change, so please check before you go, or just ask us.

Eat like a local

Kerala food to try in Trivandrum

Kerala food is built on coconut, rice, spices and the catch of the day. Small family-run eateries and "meals" restaurants are often the best and cheapest places to eat. There are several within walking distance of our house.

Appam & stew

Lacy rice pancakes with a mild coconut-milk stew. A classic breakfast.

Puttu & kadala curry

Steamed rice and coconut cylinders with a spicy black chickpea curry.

Kerala meals (sadya)

Rice with many small vegetarian dishes, served on a banana leaf. At Onam it becomes a grand feast.

Karimeen pollichathu

Pearl-spot fish marinated in spices and cooked wrapped in a banana leaf.

Parotta & curry

Flaky layered flatbread with beef, chicken or egg roast. Found in every corner eatery.

Pazham pori & chai

Ripe-banana fritters with milky tea: the classic evening snack.

Seafood in Kovalam

Choose your fish at a beachside restaurant and have it grilled or fried.

Boli & palada payasam ⭐

A Trivandrum speciality: soft, golden, sweet flatbread (boli) eaten with creamy palada payasam, a rice-flake and milk pudding. It's often the grand finale of a local sadya. Get it from Mahachips or Aanandam Take Away.

Banana chips & halwa

Fresh, coconut-oil-fried chips and sweet halwa to take home, from Chalai market.

Shopping & entertainment

🛍️ Lulu Mall Trivandrum

Kerala's biggest shopping mall, at Akkulam on the NH 66 bypass near Technopark. It's air-conditioned and easy to spend a whole afternoon in, which makes it a good choice on a hot or rainy day.

Seasons & festivals

When to visit Trivandrum

☀️ October – March

Best weather. Warm, sunny days and the calmest seas, ideal for beaches and sightseeing. Ponmudi is at its best from November to February. Book early for December and January.

🔥 April – May

Hot and humid. Plan outdoor visits for early morning and evening, and spend the middle of the day indoors or in the hills.

🌧️ June – September

Monsoon. Lush and green, with fewer crowds. It's the traditional season for Ayurvedic treatments. Seas are rough, so avoid swimming.

Festivals worth planning around

  • Onam (August/September): Kerala's harvest festival. Trivandrum hosts a week of celebrations with the city lit up, cultural programmes and a grand closing procession.
  • Attukal Pongala (February/March): one of the world's largest gatherings of women, who cook offerings in pots along the city's streets. Expect road closures that day.
  • International Film Festival of Kerala (December): held in Trivandrum, with screenings across the city's theatres.

Festival dates follow the Malayalam calendar and change every year. Ask us for this year's dates.

Good to know

Autos and app cabs are easy to find in Nettayam. For a full day out to Ponmudi, Varkala or Kanyakumari, hiring a car with a driver is the most comfortable option, and we can recommend a trusted local driver.

KSRTC buses run across the city and to most destinations here. They're cheap, but slower and busier.

From your hosts

Local tips

  • Temple etiquette: remove footwear before entering, dress modestly and check the photography rules. Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple admits Hindus only and has a strict dress code.
  • Museums and palaces close on Mondays, including Napier Museum, Kuthira Malika and Padmanabhapuram Palace.
  • Beach safety: the Arabian Sea has strong currents. Swim only at lifeguard-patrolled beaches like Kovalam, and never during the monsoon.
  • Start early: mornings are cooler and less crowded, especially for temples, Ponmudi and the backwaters.
  • Carry cash for small purchases, though UPI payments are accepted almost everywhere.
